01 / Monetary Architecture

Engineered as Neutral, Unencumbered Settlement Money

Every line of Sikka.sol is intentionally minimal. By eliminating privileged roles, discretionary minting, and transfer taxes, Sikka behaves predictably like digital commodity money.

Hard-Capped Genesis

MAX_SUPPLY is fixed at 40,000,000 * 10 ** 8 at deployment. No external or internal minting function exists anywhere in the contract.

0% Annual Inflation

Zero Admin Backdoors

No Ownable admin keys, no transfer pausing, no address blacklists, and no hidden fee setters. Every address interacts under identical consensus rules.

Immutable Execution

Voluntary Burn Deflation

Built with OpenZeppelin ERC20Burnable, enabling any holder to permanently retire tokens via burn() or burnFrom() on-chain.

Supply Strictly Decreasing

0% Transfer Friction

Transfers settle atomically without buy, sell, or wallet-to-wallet tax deductions, preserving full composability across wallets, DEXs, and payment rails.

100% Net Settlement
02 / Unit System (Siki → Sikk → Sikka)

Three-Tier Denomination & Exact BigInt Converter

With 8 decimal places (10^8 base units), Sikka scales cleanly from treasury reserves down to sub-cent retail commerce. Every conversion executes using deterministic BigInt integer math with zero floating-point drift.

100 Sikk = 1 SIKA
SIKK

Everyday retail pricing & P2P payments (0.01000000 SIKA).

10^-8 SIKA (Base)
SIKI

Smallest indivisible unit for micro-transactions (0.00000001 SIKA).
