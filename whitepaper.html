1. Abstract & Monetary Thesis

Programmable blockchains unlocked permissionless global settlement, yet the majority of fungible EVM tokens compromise the core properties of sound money through upgradeable proxies, owner-controlled minting, arbitrary transfer taxes, or freeze/blacklist hooks. When monetary policy can be altered by a single multi-signature or private key, holders no longer possess credible scarcity.

Sikka ($SIKA) returns EVM token design to strict monetary neutrality. Its smart contract fixes the maximum supply once at genesis—exactly 40,000,000 SIKA (4,000,000,000,000,000 Siki)—on top of audited OpenZeppelin ERC20 and ERC20Burnable implementations. There are no post-deployment mint functions, no administrative keys, and no transfer fees.

Sikka is currently live and publicly verified on the Ethereum Sepolia Testnet (Contract 0x4Ee5467857407FF4741271Bbba3F15C1e981eb0A, Block 12197105). The upcoming primary production deployment is planned for BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20).

2. Sound-Money Design Principles

  • Verifiable Absolute Scarcity: The constant MAX_SUPPLY (40,000,000 * 10 ** 8) is minted solely within the constructor. Because no external or internal mint pathway exists, the upper bound of 40 million SIKA is mathematically enforced by EVM bytecode.
  • Zero Privileged Control: Sikka inherits strictly from ERC20 and ERC20Burnable. There is no Ownable, AccessControl, Pausable, or blacklist state variable.
  • Zero Friction Settlement: Every transfer executes at 0% buy, sell, and peer-to-peer fee. Receivers obtain 100% of the nominal value sent.
  • Voluntary Deflationary Capability: Through ERC20Burnable, any holder or integration can provably destroy tokens via burn(uint256) or burnFrom(address, uint256), reducing circulating and total supply permanently.

3. Denomination Architecture (Siki → Sikk → Sikka)

While 18 decimals are conventional on Ethereum, high-decimal representations often create unnecessary visual friction for everyday retail payments. Sikka adopts 8 decimal places—the same precision standard proven in commodity digital money—yielding 4,000,000,000,000,000 indivisible atomic units across the entire economy.

Denomination SIKA Value Atomic Units (Siki) Operational Role
1 Sikka (SIKA) 1.00000000 100,000,000 Siki Exchange pairs, savings reserves, and treasury settlement
1 Sikk 0.01000000 1,000,000 Siki Everyday retail pricing and peer-to-peer commerce (100 Sikk = 1 Sikka)
1 Siki 0.00000001 1 Siki Smallest indivisible unit for micro-transactions and contract accounting

4. Smart Contract Architecture

Simplicity is the strongest security guarantee in smart-contract engineering. Rather than introducing custom accounting loops or tax hooks, contracts/Sikka.sol relies exclusively on OpenZeppelin Contracts v5.5.0, overriding only decimals() to return 8.
